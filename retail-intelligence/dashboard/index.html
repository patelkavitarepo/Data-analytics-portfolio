Decision memo

Preliminary interpretation
  1. Do not report +2.90% as certified sales growth; it measures the provisional S2 positive-priced source-line scenario.
  2. Show both +2.90% and +0.77% sensitivity. The top end of the invoice distribution changes the narrative.
  3. Verify whether product mix, quantities, and observed gross per unit explain the difference. The SKU decomposition is a separate evidence gate—not assumed here.
  4. Keep incomplete December 2011 outside matched full-month year-over-year claims; maintain refund and zero-price ambiguity.
