# Project 007 — FI-2010 alternative benchmark evidence review
**Review date:** November 11, 2026. **Status: candidate source, not an acquired/verified source snapshot.**

## Why consider FI-2010

The FI-2010 benchmark by Ntakaris, Magris, Kanniainen, Gabbouj and Iosifidis, *Benchmark Dataset for Mid-Price Forecasting of Limit Order Book Data with Machine Learning Methods* (Journal of Forecasting 2018), is a standard short historical benchmark of five stocks traded on Nasdaq Nordic during 10 days in June 2010. Original catalog: https://etsin.fairdata.fi/dataset/73eb48d7-4dbc-4a10-a52a-da745b47a649 . Paper: https://doi.org/10.1002/for.2543 . The article and associated references report the NoAuction/DecPre format as a normalized 149-by-N matrix of 144 source-processed features followed by five 3-class future-mid-price target rows (1, 2, 3, 5, 10 sampled-observation horizons; class 1 up, 2 stationary, 3 down). Predefined anchored calendar folds identify the first k days of training and day k+1 for testing.

A secondary dataset catalog/research reproduction identifies a **CC BY 4.0** data license, which may allow reuse and publication with attribution: https://github.com/d9nk/deeplob-project and https://jeffhub.ai/adapters/trading-desk . **But the official Fairdata dataset license/access metadata were not independently retrievable here and the official files were not downloaded.** Do not equate third-party descriptions with independently verified applicable conditions.

## What is and is not supported

- What this supports once authorized: evaluating a simple, reproducible **benchmark-label** classifier on the prescribed day-8 fold (without touching day 9/10); comparing accuracy and macro-F1 to later models under the same measurement policy.
- This source **does not** expose raw exchange order message flow, original elapsed time or a trustworthy absolute displayed share count/price basis after normalization. Do not calculate actual USD spreads, queue positions, order-flow arrival rates, time-based execution costs or P&L.
- Labels are originally generated by the dataset publisher and **have not been reconstructed independently**. No claim that upstream horizon calculations exclude adjacent-session contamination is established. A day-8 historical comparison is *internal exploratory benchmark validation*, not pristine holdout; day 9/10 are reserved and unread.
- FI-2010's 2010 sample is too short and old to provide evidence of modern or out-of-regime trading viability. Features may reflect pooled stocks, historical sampling and class-dependence effects.
- Exact original data hashes, the archive download URL, source files, license statement and potential attribution text remain to be validated before any public empirical scores are issued.
- Never upload cached original data or labels from third-party mirrors to this repository merely because they can be accessed.

## Implemented protocol — code only

`microstructure/fi2010_baseline.py` checks 149-row structure and numeric finiteness; checks labels restricted to 1,2,3 for **all five** horizons; keeps only three-class counts; selects a training-prior constant predictor from `Train_Dst_NoAuction_DecPre_CF_7.txt`; computes day-8 accuracy and macro-F1 against `Test_Dst_NoAuction_DecPre_CF_7.txt`; hashes files before and after. It refuses other fold filenames and never opens the day-9/day-10 files. It treats prior aggregate labels as benchmark-provided and outputs only a local, ignored JSON report. **A filename match is not provenance certification.** Synthetic tests use small invented 149-row fixtures.

## Admission criteria before any empirical performance claim

1. Retrieve Fairdata publisher record and record its own license metadata, access conditions, official citation, and any restrictions, distinct from third-party repositories.
2. Acquire files only through entitled access, record download path and SHA256, confirm matrix dimensions and source-provided definitions.
3. Audit fold boundaries and whether benchmark labels use future observations beyond training day. If unresolved, report potential label-boundary contamination explicitly and do not describe a leakage-clean validation.
4. Obtain human interpretation of first actual dataset diagnostics and agree on the label, split and comparison before selecting a model.
5. Keep actual-data score reports outside Git until the publication and attribution basis has been verified; never report them as live HFT performance.
